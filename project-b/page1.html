Enhanced Mind Interface (EMI)

The Enhanced Mind Interface (EMI) is a brain chip, one that would be implanted in the host's cerebrum, the area of the brain responsible for communication and abstract thought. The medical procedure required for insertion is not relatively invasive and has had massive success in preliminary testing.

For the fair sum of $20,000 US dollars, you can develop the ability to communicate without moving a single muscle, regardless of physical location, at the blink of a thought. (EMI Inc. is not responsible for any permanent injury and/or death to chip host)
