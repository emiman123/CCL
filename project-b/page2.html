Capability of the EMI

The EMI's main capability is telepathic communication; with the chip in place, the user can simply think of what they want to say and another individual hosting the EMI will receive it within 0.001 nanoseconds, regardless of how far apart they are. This is only the beginning, however; we hope our chip will one day allow users with paralysis disorders to regain movement and be able to live more fulfilling lives as a result.
